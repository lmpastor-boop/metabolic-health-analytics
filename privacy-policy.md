# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

## Overview

This application is part of a personal data analytics project that explores relationships between physical activity, body composition, and metabolic health.

The application may access Fitbit data such as activity, heart rate, and sleep metrics for research and educational purposes.

## Data Collected

If you authorize the application through Fitbit, the following types of data may be accessed:

- Daily step count
- Heart rate data
- Sleep duration and sleep stages
- Active minutes and exercise sessions
- Calories burned

The application may also analyze publicly available health datasets such as NHANES for comparison and modeling purposes.

## How Data Is Used

Collected data is used only for:

- health analytics research
- educational data science projects
- exploring relationships between physical activity and metabolic health indicators

The data is not used for advertising or commercial purposes.

## Data Sharing

This application does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

Any results produced by the project are aggregated and anonymized.

## Data Storage

Authorized Fitbit data may be temporarily stored in a secure database for analysis. Data is used only for the duration of the research project.

## Revoking Access

Users can revoke access to their Fitbit data at any time through their Fitbit account settings.

## Medical Disclaimer

This project is intended for educational and research purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
